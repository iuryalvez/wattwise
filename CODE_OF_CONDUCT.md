# Code of conduct

WattWise welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces maintained by the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific. Discuss an idea or implementation without attacking its author.
- Welcome newcomers, explain assumptions, and offer actionable feedback.
- Respect people's names, identities, privacy, and communication preferences.
- Accept corrections and take responsibility for the effect of your words.
- Keep discussion relevant and leave room for others to participate.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, sharing private information without permission, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable. Disagreement and constructive criticism are welcome; repeated disruption after moderation is not.

## Reporting and moderation

The current project maintainer is [@rafaelsilvabr](https://github.com/rafaelsilvabr). Request moderation by mentioning the maintainer in the affected thread when it is safe to do so. For sensitive reports, use a private contact method the maintainer publishes on their GitHub profile. If none is available, open a brief issue titled "Private moderation contact requested" without names, screenshots, or incident details so a private channel can be arranged.

Maintainers should protect the reporter's privacy, listen to the people involved, and explain moderation decisions where possible without exposing private information. Responses may include requesting an edit, removing content, warning a participant, limiting participation, or blocking someone from project spaces. The response should reflect the severity and pattern of behavior. Maintainers are also expected to follow this policy.

If the report concerns the sole maintainer or platform-level abuse, GitHub's own reporting tools remain available. This policy does not promise confidentiality from the platform or a fixed response time.
